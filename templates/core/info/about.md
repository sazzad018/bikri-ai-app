# About CholbeAI

**CholbeAI** is an AI-powered social media automation platform designed to help businesses handle customer conversations effortlessly on **Facebook Messenger** and **WhatsApp Business**.

## What We Do

Managing customer messages across multiple platforms can be overwhelming. CholbeAI gives you an **intelligent AI agent** that automatically replies to your customers, answers their questions, shares product information, and even takes orders — so you never miss a sale.

## Key Features

- **24/7 AI Chat Agent** — An intelligent assistant that replies to customer messages on Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp around the clock.
- **Smart Product Catalog** — Upload your products and let the AI recommend and share them with customers during conversations.
- **Order Placement via Chat** — Customers can place orders directly through the AI chat without leaving Messenger or WhatsApp.
- **Credit-Based Pricing** — Pay only for what you use. Credits are deducted per conversation handled by the AI.
- **Unified Dashboard** — Manage all your conversations, products, and orders from a single, easy-to-use dashboard.
- **No Coding Required** — Connect your Facebook Page and WhatsApp Business account in just a few clicks.

## How It Works

1. **Connect Your Accounts** — Link your Facebook Page and WhatsApp Business number to CholbeAI.
2. **Set Up Your AI** — Customize your AI agent's tone, behavior, and product knowledge.
3. **Let It Run** — The AI agent automatically responds to incoming messages, shares products, and handles orders.

## Who Is This For?

CholbeAI is built for:

- **Small and medium businesses** selling products via social media
- **Online shops** looking to automate customer inquiries
- **Service providers** who receive frequent inquiries on Messenger or WhatsApp
- **Anyone** who wants to stop manually replying to the same customer questions over and over

## Our Mission

Our mission is to make AI-powered customer communication accessible, affordable, and effortless for businesses of all sizes in Bangladesh.
