## Credit Purchases

{platform_name} operates on a **credit-based** system. You purchase credits in packages, and credits are deducted each time the AI agent handles a customer conversation on your behalf.

## Eligibility for Refunds

Refund requests are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. You may be eligible for a refund under the following conditions:

### Full Refund

You may request a **full refund** if:

- The refund request is made within **{full_refund_hours} hours** of the credit purchase.
- You have **not** used any credits from the purchased package.
- The payment can be verified in our system.

### Partial Refund

You may request a **partial refund** if:

- The refund request is made within **{partial_refund_days} days** of the credit purchase.
- Only a portion of the purchased credits has been used.
- The refund amount will be calculated as: `{purchase_amount} - ({used_credits} × {credit_unit_price})`

### Service Failure

You may request a full or partial refund if:

- The AI agent failed to respond to customer messages due to a **platform-wide outage** lasting more than **{outage_threshold_hours} hours**.
- A documented **technical bug** prevented the core functionality from working.
- The issue was **reported promptly** and confirmed by our support team.

## Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will **not** be issued in the following cases:

- More than **{refund_deadline_days} days** have passed since the purchase date.
- Credits were consumed due to your **own misconfiguration** of the AI agent or business profile.
- You experienced dissatisfaction with the **quality of AI responses**, which can be improved through prompt customization (our support team can assist).
- Credits were used for conversations that originated from **your own test messages**.
- You purchased the wrong package by mistake and **failed to contact us promptly**.
- The refund request is related to a **change of mind** after the eligible refund window.

## How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, follow these steps:

1. **Contact Support** — Send an email to **{support_email}** with the subject line "Refund Request".
2. **Provide Details** — Include your registered email, purchase date, amount, and the reason for your request.
3. **Verification** — Our team will verify your purchase and credit usage within **{verification_days} business days**.
4. **Resolution** — If approved, the refund will be processed to your original payment method within **{processing_days} business days**.

## Refund Method

All approved refunds will be issued to the **original payment method** used during the credit purchase. We do not issue cash refunds or refunds to alternative payment methods.
